shift once the arrival date and launch date are actually confirmed.

## The Critical Path

The tasks that can actually delay or prevent an October launch, in the order they block each
other. Packaging is included here — it was previously treated as parallel work, but printed-box
lead time and packaging finalization can independently push the launch date.

1. **Pre-payment verification gate.** Before releasing the remaining supplier balance, obtain and
   review the full checklist in Section A below. This does not yet exist.
2. **Release the remaining supplier balance**, once the verification gate is passed — not before,
   and not simply "whenever we choose."
3. **Supplier confirms dispatch date, carrier, and estimated arrival.** Not yet confirmed.
4. **Transit + customs clearance to arrival in Israel.**
5. **Arrival QC on all 120 units**, including re-verifying Raw Pearl Necklace length and Raw Pearl
   Bracelet plating physically (plating cannot be reliably confirmed by visual inspection alone —
   see Section B).
6. **Packaging finalized in parallel**: contents per product/order, bundle packaging rule, box
   dimensions/artwork, printed price, proof approval, vendor production time, order placed,
   received and inspected before launch.
7. **Shipping method decided** (home delivery vs. pickup vs. both) — unblocks checkout.
8. **Payments and checkout fully tested**: gateway activated, fee structure confirmed, receipts
   configured, successful/declined payments tested, refunds tested, transactional emails tested,
   one complete end-to-end test order run.
9. **Website testing completed** (Hebrew/RTL, mobile/desktop, pricing accuracy, bundle inventory
   sync, stock limits, shipping thresholds, policies, broken links).
10. **Launch date set**, worked backward from confirmed arrival + QC + packaging + content
    readiness.
11. **Go/no-go checklist passed** the day before launch.
